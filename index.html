Land cover

From registration: a covenant's land joins at the last LCDB date in or before the year it was registered (1996 for older covenants), so only change from then on is shown, and later columns are taller as covenants are added. From 1996 shows every covenant's land at every date, including change before it was covenanted.

Period: all six dates, one period between two dates, or straight from each covenant's start (registration, or 1996) to 2023. A single period is the easiest to read. Change only hides land that stayed in the same class, so the changes can be seen; band widths are then on a different scale from All land. In Grouped view, a change between two classes in the same group counts as staying. Changes smaller than 1% of the largest change in view aren't drawn (the line above the graph says how much that leaves out); they're still in the totals and the CSVs, and nothing is hidden for a single covenant.

LCDB maps at about 1 ha, and shows only the cover at each date: small clearings, edges, and anything that changed and recovered between dates won't show. Thin changes along a boundary can come from linework rather than change on the ground.
